 The **Patents (Amendment) Rules, 2025** have been notified (Gazette dated Nov 25, 2025). Key highlight: A new Chapter XIV-A introduces streamlined electronic procedures for adjudicating penalties & appeals under Sections 120, 122 & 123 of the Patents Act (e.g., unauthorized patent claims, non-furnishing info, or practice by unregistered agents).  Features include: - E-filing complaints (Form 32) - Time-bound inquiries (3 months) - Appeals (Form 33, 60 days) - Digital orders & transparency  This boosts efficiency & decriminalizes minor contraventions. Great step for innovators! 🚀
